## Purpose
AI-B01: expected hourly OER from FFB quality and process conditions, with a 90% interval and a naive baseline; multi-hour forecasts hold the latest conditions constant.

## Input features
`ffb_ripe_pct`, `recv_unripe_pct_12h`, `recv_harvest_to_mill_h_12h`, `avg_wait_to_process_h`, `ffb_waiting_t`, `sterilizer_cycle_min`, `steam_pressure_bar`, `boiler_steam_t`, `digester_temp_c`, `clarifier_temp_c`, `press_load_a`, `press_cone_pressure_bar`, `press_vib_max`, `press_vib_mean`, `press_cur_max`, `throughput_tph`, `running_minutes`, `hour_local`, `weekday_local`, `day_of_year`, `ffb_ripe_pct_d24`, `recv_unripe_pct_12h_d24`, `recv_harvest_to_mill_h_12h_d24`, `avg_wait_to_process_h_d24`, `sterilizer_cycle_min_d24`, `steam_pressure_bar_d24`, `digester_temp_c_d24`, `clarifier_temp_c_d24`, `press_vib_max_d24`, `press_cur_max_d24`, `throughput_tph_d24`, `oer_level_lag24`

## Training data
process_hourly running hours (>= 30 min, FFB processed > 0) + 12 h delivery quality + press vibration/current (hourly means); train 2026-07-24..10-14 local.

## Synthetic-data assumptions
- Synthetic Demonstration Data from a fictional mill ('Demo Palm Mill A'); 120 days, one line; no company data.
- We generated the data ourselves, so the generator's drivers are fully observable; real plants have unmeasured drivers, sensor drift and labelling gaps - expect worse results on real data.
- Temporal split: train < 2026-10-15 00:00 Asia/Bangkok, test >= it. injected_events (ground truth) are used only for evaluation and for tuning thresholds on the training period.
- Hourly OER = deterministic loss model + hourly and daily noise in the generator (DATA_GENERATION.md section 4).

## Evaluation method
3 expanding-window folds inside the training period select the served candidate; every candidate is then scored once on the test period vs persistence, seasonal-naive 24 h and 24 h rolling-mean baselines (MAE/RMSE). Split-conformal 90% interval (calibrated on the last 14 train days) with measured test coverage; permutation importance; driver-group ablation; walk-forward per-scenario error.

## Limitations
- Does not beat the 24 h rolling-mean baseline on the test period (see metrics) - hourly OER is noise-dominated.
- Shift (8 h) and day granularity were also evaluated: neither beats its best naive baseline (see granularity table). The useful output is the day-level nowcast residual (actual - expected) as a flag, not the forecast.
- Out-of-sample driver attribution of OER_DEGRADATION was unreliable (wrong signs for ripe %, clarifier temperature, sterilizer cycle: these drivers barely varied before the episode); the in-sample view attributes it plausibly.
- Trees cannot extrapolate to driver values never seen in training (e.g. the 66-min sterilizer cycle).
- Multi-hour forecast = persistence of conditions; the interval does not widen with horizon.
- day_of_year in the test period lies beyond the training range; trees treat it as the last seen value, and local explanations can show a large seasonality contribution that is an extrapolation artefact.

## Known failure modes
- Large multi-day OER shifts are absorbed by the lagged-level feature with ~1 day delay.
- Stopped / partially running hours are excluded; start-up hours are noisy.

## What is NOT claimed
- No real-plant accuracy. No calibrated probability. Anomalies are possible contributing factors, never proven causes; logistics flags are review requests, never accusations.
