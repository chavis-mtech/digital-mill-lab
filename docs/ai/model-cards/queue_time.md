## Purpose
AI-D01: predicted arrival-to-weigh-in wait for trucks in the queue (with P10-P90 band).

## Input features
`trucks_ahead`, `trucks_in_yard`, `arrivals_last_60m`, `weigh_ins_last_60m`, `hour_local`, `weekday_local`, `truck_class`

## Training data
truck_trips (yard state at arrival computed from earlier events only).

## Synthetic-data assumptions
- Synthetic Demonstration Data from a fictional mill ('Demo Palm Mill A'); 120 days, one line; no company data.
- We generated the data ourselves, so the generator's drivers are fully observable; real plants have unmeasured drivers, sensor drift and labelling gaps - expect worse results on real data.
- Temporal split: train < 2026-10-15 00:00 Asia/Bangkok, test >= it. injected_events (ground truth) are used only for evaluation and for tuning thresholds on the training period.
- Queue model in the generator is interval booking, FIFO-ish, with known outages.

## Evaluation method
Test MAE/RMSE vs a queue-drain baseline (gate time + trucks ahead / recent weigh-in rate) and vs the training median; separate MAE in the congestion window; P10-P90 coverage.

## Limitations
- Gain over the drain-time baseline is marginal (see metrics).
- No appointment system or driver behaviour.

## Known failure modes

## What is NOT claimed
