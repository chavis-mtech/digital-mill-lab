**Task:** anomaly

## Purpose
AI-B02: flag hours where the plant process state is unusual (OER below expected, steam pressure, processing rate, sterilizer cycle, temperatures, oil loss, kWh/t, unplanned downtime) with evidence and possible factors.

## Input features
`oer_residual_4h`, `steam_pressure_bar`, `throughput_tph`, `sterilizer_cycle_min`, `digester_temp_c`, `clarifier_temp_c`, `oil_loss_total_pct`, `kwh_per_t`, `unplanned_downtime_min`

## Training data
Hourly process signals; baselines are expanding median/MAD of each signal's own history (data older than 2 days).

## Synthetic-data assumptions
- Synthetic Demonstration Data from a fictional mill ('Demo Palm Mill A'); 120 days, one line; no company data.
- We generated the data ourselves, so the generator's drivers are fully observable; real plants have unmeasured drivers, sensor drift and labelling gaps - expect worse results on real data.
- Temporal split: train < 2026-10-15 00:00 Asia/Bangkok, test >= it. injected_events (ground truth) are used only for evaluation and for tuning thresholds on the training period.
- The Sunday 05:00-13:00 planned stop is plant schedule knowledge and is not treated as downtime.

## Evaluation method
Event-level precision (detections overlapping any injected label), recall over production/quality labels + the STEAM driver, false positives per day, detection latency (detected_at - label start), per scenario; threshold tuned on the training period only; IsolationForest and an OER-residual-only rule as comparators.

## Limitations
- Short (< 1 h) breakdowns are missed by the 2-consecutive-hour rule.
- PRESS_VIBRATION_ANOMALY moves plant OER only ~0.3 pp - production signals do not see it; equipment detection does.
- Small number of test labels (7): metrics have wide uncertainty.

## Known failure modes
- Signals with very stable history (MAD floor) can over-react to small genuine changes.
- A long anomaly older than 2 days slowly enters the expanding baseline only if it lasts weeks.

## What is NOT claimed
- No real-plant accuracy. No calibrated probability. Anomalies are possible contributing factors, never proven causes; logistics flags are review requests, never accusations.
