## Training data
Hourly means of 80+ sensors on steady-running hours (current above 25% of normal max this hour and the previous one).

## Synthetic-data assumptions
- Synthetic Demonstration Data from a fictional mill ('Demo Palm Mill A'); 120 days, one line; no company data.
- We generated the data ourselves, so the generator's drivers are fully observable; real plants have unmeasured drivers, sensor drift and labelling gaps - expect worse results on real data.
- Temporal split: train < 2026-10-15 00:00 Asia/Bangkok, test >= it. injected_events (ground truth) are used only for evaluation and for tuning thresholds on the training period.
- Degradation shapes in the generator are prescribed curves (power laws): lead times say nothing about real bearings.

## Evaluation method
Event-level metrics vs injected equipment labels (same equipment, 12 h tolerance); PRS-03 lead time vs the seeded warning and critical threshold alarms; rule alarms and a per-equipment IsolationForest as comparators.

## Limitations
- A stopped machine (breakdown) is not an anomaly in its sensors - breakdowns are visible from state/downtime, not here.
- A 30-min sensor spike is smoothed away by the 3 h mean (missed in test).
- Low current is ignored (unloaded/idle running is an energy question).

## Known failure modes
- Process-driven changes (e.g. low steam) show up on several machines at once.
- Very slow drifts over months would enter the expanding baseline.

## What is NOT claimed
- No real-plant accuracy. No calibrated probability. Anomalies are possible contributing factors, never proven causes; logistics flags are review requests, never accusations.
