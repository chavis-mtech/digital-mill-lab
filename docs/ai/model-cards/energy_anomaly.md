## Purpose
AI-E02: energy use above the production-adjusted expectation (walk-forward model) with idle-running evidence.

## Input features
`ffb_processed_t`, `running_minutes`, `throughput_tph`, `hour_local`, `weekday_local`, `electricity_kwh (observed, compared with expectation)`

## Training data
Walk-forward expected kWh (weekly refits on past data) and robust z of the residual.

## Synthetic-data assumptions
- Synthetic Demonstration Data from a fictional mill ('Demo Palm Mill A'); 120 days, one line; no company data.
- We generated the data ourselves, so the generator's drivers are fully observable; real plants have unmeasured drivers, sensor drift and labelling gaps - expect worse results on real data.
- Temporal split: train < 2026-10-15 00:00 Asia/Bangkok, test >= it. injected_events (ground truth) are used only for evaluation and for tuning thresholds on the training period.
- ENERGY_EFFICIENCY_DROP lies in the training period; the test period has one energy label (an idle-running night).

## Evaluation method
Event-level metrics vs injected energy labels; comparator = the plant's daily 18.5 kWh/t rule.
